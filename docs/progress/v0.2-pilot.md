## Known Limitations

- A0 is preflight only. It intentionally does not decode frames, execute perception, create
  cases, call VLMs, or publish reports.
- `configs/pilot.yaml` names the future `local-worker` boundary, but its detector model is
  explicitly `pilot-detector-unconfigured` until A1 records a licensed model manifest.
- `report` and `review` are discoverable but return `STAGE_NOT_AVAILABLE` until A4.
- Only `PPE_GOGGLES_MISSING` is accepted for the alpha1 pilot; the presence rule remains B1.
- No accuracy, latency, throughput, or cost claim has been made.
