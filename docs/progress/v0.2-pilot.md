## Known Limitations

- A1 preview approval and A2 crop inspection cover three owner-supplied, visibly AI-generated
  sample videos, but there is no real-camera site acceptance. These samples and the geometric
  smoke cannot establish real-site detector quality and are not ground truth.
- Pilot stream timing is a caller-attested CFR declaration derived from bounded decoder metadata;
  variable-frame-rate source files require normalization or a future trusted timing sidecar.
- The preview is a non-authoritative debugging artifact and may contain people from the selected
  local input. It remains under the ignored local `runs/` boundary.
- `report` and `review` are discoverable but return `STAGE_NOT_AVAILABLE` until A4; `screen` is now
  available only when the run manifest and current config bind the same external provider identity.
- Only `PPE_GOGGLES_MISSING` is accepted for the alpha1 pilot; the presence rule remains B1.
- Pose-backed temporal crops are cloud-eligible; panorama and subject crops remain local-only.
  The sample inputs are still visibly AI-generated and cannot establish real-site privacy or model
  performance.
- No accuracy, latency, throughput, or cost claim has been made.
